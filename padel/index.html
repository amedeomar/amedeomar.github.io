Padel Companion

A padel scoreboard for Amazfit watches running Zepp OS.

Padel Companion keeps the score of a padel match on your wrist: points, games and sets, with the four ways a 40-40 can be settled: advantage, star point, silver point and golden point. One tap scores for the blue team, one for the gold team, and a long press undoes the last point.

It runs on Amazfit Bip Max, Bip 6 and Active 2 Square, and is installed from the Zepp app store.

Matches on your Google Sheet

Optionally, you can sign in with Google from the app's settings page in the Zepp phone app. The app then creates a single spreadsheet in your Google Drive, named Padel Companion — Matches, and writes one row per match into it: date, duration, sets, games, points, break points and deciding points. Columns for the two team names, venue and notes are left for you to fill in, and the app never touches them.

Signing in is not required. Without it the app works exactly as before, entirely on the watch.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere except to your own Google Drive, and only if you ask it to. The details are in the privacy policy.
