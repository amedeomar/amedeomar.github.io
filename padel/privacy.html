Privacy Policy

Padel Companion — last updated 30 August 2026

Padel Companion has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer. The only place your data can go is your own Google Drive, and only if you choose to connect it.

What the app stores on the watch

The match in progress — points, games, sets and their timings — is written to the watch's local storage, so that it survives closing the app or restarting the watch. It is overwritten when a new match starts, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Watch permissions

  • Local storage — to save the match in progress, as described above.
  • Device information — to read the screen size at startup, so the layout suits the model in use. The value is neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Bluetooth messaging — to hand finished matches to the Zepp app on your phone, which is the only part of the chain that has network access.

The app reads no heart rate, no step count, no GPS position and no other sensor data. Besides the screen, the only hardware it uses is the vibration motor.

Connecting Google Drive, if you want to

From the app's settings page inside the Zepp phone app you may sign in with Google. The app requests a single permission, drive.file, which grants access only to the files the app itself creates — the rest of your Drive stays invisible to it, including files you created and files created by other apps.

With that permission the app creates one spreadsheet, named Padel Companion — Matches, and writes your match results into it: date, duration, sets, games, points, break points and the scoring mode. The spreadsheet is yours, in your Drive, and you may edit or delete it at any time.

Where the Google credentials live

The access and refresh tokens issued by Google are stored by the Zepp app on your phone, in the storage reserved for this app's settings, and are used only to talk to Google's own APIs. They are never sent to the developer, never leave your phone for anywhere else, and are erased when you press Disconnect or uninstall the app. The watch never sees them.

Signing in opens Google's own consent screen through a redirect page hosted on GitHub Pages at amedeomar.github.io. That page only forwards the request to Google; it never sees your account, your password or any token. Like any web page, it is served by GitHub, whose own logging is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Disconnect in the app's settings page removes the stored tokens from your phone and stops all uploads.
  • Deleting the spreadsheet from your Google Drive deletes the match data, which exists nowhere else.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account permissions.

Children

The app is a scoreboard for a racket sport. It has no user accounts of its own, no messaging and no user-generated content, and it collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

Should a future version ever collect or transmit anything not described here, this page will be updated before that version is published.
